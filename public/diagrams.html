6. Activity Diagram: Smart Irrigation Decision Logic

Outlines the condition branch analysis: IoT moisture sensing, rain forecast gating (≥ 60% probability), heatwave volume compensation, and automated valve actuation scheduling. 

flowchart TD
    START([● Start: Scheduled Irrigation Cycle]) --> FETCH_TELEMETRY["Acquire Soil Moisture & Temperature
(IoT Telemetry or Manual Log)"]
    
    FETCH_TELEMETRY --> FETCH_WEATHER["Fetch 7-Day Local Weather Forecast
from Open-Meteo API"]
    
    FETCH_WEATHER --> CHECK_RAIN{"Is Rain Probability >= 60%
in the next 24 Hours?"}
    
    CHECK_RAIN -- Yes --> LOG_SKIP["Log Decision: SKIPPED (Rain Expected)"]
    LOG_SKIP --> ALERT_RAIN["Dispatch Alert: 'Rain Expected. Save Water'"]
    ALERT_RAIN --> END_STATE([● End Cycle])

    CHECK_RAIN -- No --> CHECK_MOISTURE{"Is Soil Moisture < Threshold?
(e.g., < 35% for Tomato)"}
    
    CHECK_MOISTURE -- No: Moisture Adequate --> LOG_OPTIMAL["Log State: MOISTURE_OPTIMAL"]
    LOG_OPTIMAL --> END_STATE

    CHECK_MOISTURE -- Yes: Dry Soil --> CALC_BASE["Calculate Base Water Requirement
(Acres × 500 Litres)"]
    
    CALC_BASE --> CHECK_HEAT{"Is Ambient Temp >= 32°C?"}
    
    CHECK_HEAT -- Yes --> ADD_HEAT["Scale Volume by +20%
(Evapotranspiration Offset)"]
    CHECK_HEAT -- No --> COMPOSE_PLAN["Schedule Drip Irrigation:
Early Morning (06:00) or Evening (18:00)"]
    
    ADD_HEAT --> COMPOSE_PLAN
    COMPOSE_PLAN --> RECORD_LOG["Persist Record in Database:
IrrigationLog & Recommendation"]
    RECORD_LOG --> NOTIFY_FARMER["Deliver Notification to Farmer"]
    NOTIFY_FARMER --> END_STATE
